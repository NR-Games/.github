# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in NR Games spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, store reviews we answer and email — follows these rules.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep to the topic and give facts: versions, steps, screenshots.
- Accept decisions of the maintainers.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, discrimination of any kind.
- Spam, advertising, links to cheats, cracked builds or pirated copies.
- Publishing other people's private data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers remove content that breaks these rules and may block the author from the organization.
Report a problem to rybak.nekyadnex.ru@gmail.com with the subject `CONDUCT`.
