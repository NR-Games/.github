# Security policy

This policy covers every repository, game and app published by NR Games.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Send the report privately to **rybak.nekyadnex.ru@gmail.com** with the subject `SECURITY: <product>`.
Do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion for a vulnerability.

Include:

- the product and version (for a game: the version shown in Settings, and the store you installed it from);
- what an attacker can do and the steps to reproduce it;
- any proof of concept, logs or screenshots.

## What happens next

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| We confirm we received the report | 3 business days |
| We confirm or reject the issue | 10 business days |
| A fix ships in a store update | as fast as the store review allows |

We credit reporters in the release notes unless you ask us not to.

## Scope

In scope: our games and apps from Google Play, Huawei AppGallery and RuStore, our websites, and the code in this organization.
Out of scope: issues in third-party stores, ad networks or operating systems — report those to their owners.
